<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Build Ticket Service and Semantic Grouping Logic</name>
  <files>server/services/ticketService.js</files>
  <action>
    Create `server/services/ticketService.js` implementing:
    - `getScopedTicket(ticketId, sessionUser)`: fetches ticket and verifies access: customer must match `createdBy` and `orgId`; specialist must match `assignedTo`; supervisor/knowledge manager have staff oversight.
    - `createTicket(orgId, userId, subject, question)`: creates ticket with initial message, detects feature keyword (e.g. export, login), and correlates matching active public incidents (per GROUP-01).
    - `listTickets(sessionUser)`: queries tickets filtered strictly by customer org/author or specialist assignment.
    - `updateTicketStatus(ticket, patch)`: updates status/priority with audit fields.
    - `resolveTicket(ticket, resolution, specialistId)`: sets resolution text and status='resolved'.
    - `escalateTicket(ticket, reason)`: sets status='escalated' and logs escalationReason.
    - `assignTicket(ticket, specialistId, supervisorId)`: updates assignedTo and creates active `Assignment` record.
    - `getRelatedIssues(ticketId, sessionUser)`: correlates tickets by feature tag and incident matching without exposing customer names or conversation bodies across orgs (per GROUP-02).
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>node -e "import('./server/services/ticketService.js').then(() => console.log('Ticket service loaded successfully'))"</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>Ticket business logic handles creation, scoping, escalation, assignment, and privacy-preserving grouping.</done>
</task>
